# Reimbursement policy

- Meals are reimbursable up to 80 CAD with a receipt.
- Transit is reimbursable up to 120 CAD with a receipt.
- Software is reimbursable up to 300 CAD only when finance_approved is true.
- Missing receipts must be denied even if the manager approved the request.
- Manager approval alone is not sufficient for software above 300 CAD.
